About BodyBlueprint

One place for your body metrics, hydration and sleep.

What is BodyBlueprint?

BodyBlueprint puts your body metrics, hydration and sleep in one place, so you can see how they affect each other and get simple daily guidance.

How to use it

  1. Enter your height, weight, age and sex on the BMI & BMR page.
  2. Set a daily water goal and add what you drink on the Water page.
  3. Log your bedtime, wake-up time and sleep quality on the Sleep page.
  4. Check your score and suggestions on the Wellness page.

What the numbers mean

BMI compares your weight to your height.

BMR is the energy your body uses at rest each day (Mifflin-St Jeor equation).

Wellness Score combines BMI (30%), hydration (35%) and sleep (35%) into one score out of 100.

Quick answers

Is BMI a perfect measure of health?

No. It does not separate muscle from fat, so athletes and older adults may get misleading results. Treat it as one indicator.

Why is my water goal based on weight?

Larger bodies generally need more fluid. Climate, activity and health also matter, so adjust the goal to suit you.

Does less sleep affect my wellness score?

Yes. Sleep makes up 35% of the score, and regularly sleeping under 7 hours lowers it.
